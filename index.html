What to Prepare Essential checklist before you go

💳 Finance & Payment

Octopus Card — Most Important! Works everywhere: MTR, buses, convenience stores, local restaurants. Download "Octopus for Tourists" app and link to Apple Pay on iPhone. No physical card needed!
Cash (HKD) Some small local restaurants are cash-only. Carry some just in case.
Travel Card (YouTrip / Planet / SCB Planet) Better exchange rates than cash. Tap to pay at malls.

📱 Internet & Apps

SIM / eSIM Buy before leaving Thailand (Sim2Fly or Travel Sim). Or buy CSL at HK Airport.
MTR Mobile Train routes and fares
Hong Kong Disneyland App Real-time wait times, park map, show & fireworks schedules
Google Maps Essential for navigating HK's many alleyways
Citybus / HKTaxi Bus schedules or call a taxi

🔌 Power & Essentials

Type G Power Adapter Hong Kong uses UK-style 3-pin square plugs. Pack a Universal Adapter!
Power Bank Essential for a full day at Disneyland or walking around
Comfortable Shoes Expect 15,000–20,000+ steps on Disneyland day. Choose wisely!
Compact Umbrella / Rain Jacket HK weather is unpredictable — can be sunny or rainy in the same day

🎡 Disneyland Pre-checks

Buy Tickets via Klook Purchase in advance for the best price
Make Park Reservation Reserve your entry date online after purchasing tickets
Empty Water Bottle Drinks inside are expensive. Refill free at Water Stations (usually near restrooms)
Hat & Sunscreen Lots of outdoor time at the park

🚫 Disneyland Prohibited Items

Leave these at the hotel to avoid problems at the entrance

✗
Selfie Stick / Camera Tripod Not permitted inside for safety reasons
✗
Oversized Bags (>56×36×23 cm) Must be checked at luggage storage outside the park
✗
Full Meals / Packed Food Small snacks & small water bottles OK, but no packed meals
✗
Alcoholic Beverages Alcohol is not permitted inside Hong Kong Disneyland

Quick Travel Tips Make the most of your Hong Kong trip

🃏 Octopus Card = Life

Get this first thing at the airport. Link it to Apple Pay via the "Octopus for Tourists" app. It works on MTR, buses, taxis, convenience stores, and even some restaurants.

🚇 MTR is King

Hong Kong's MTR is one of the world's best metro systems — fast, clean, and air-conditioned. Always check the app for the quickest route. Lines are colour-coded.

🍜 Eat Like a Local

Some of the best food is in tiny, unassuming restaurants. Don't be afraid to join a queue — if locals are waiting, it's probably worth it!

☔ Weather is Wild

March in HK can be warm and humid, but also rainy. Always carry a compact umbrella. Layer up if heading to The Peak — it can be cooler up there.

📸 Best Photo Spots

Kennedy Town basketball court (morning), Choi Hung Estate rooftop (morning/afternoon), The Peak Tram (golden hour), Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade (Victoria Harbour night view).

💰 Dining Budget

Budget ~HKD 50–80 for local breakfast (congee, dim sum), ~HKD 80–150 for lunch, and ~HKD 100–200 for dinner. Roast goose restaurants are worth every dollar!
